GORKHA TV BRAND PACK v1.0
=========================
Master brand: GORKHA TV. Products: CHIMAL (films), KHABAR (news), SWARA (music), UKAALI (reels).

01-symbols      Symbol-only marks: color (on dark), color-onlight, white, black + 1024px PNG
02-wordmarks    Wordmarks (outlined): snow (on dark), ink (on light), Devanagari
03-lockups      Horizontal + vertical lockups: ondark, onlight, mono-white, mono-black (+ PNG previews)
04-app-icons    Store icons (1024/512/192 PNG, full-bleed square), rounded previews, Android adaptive foregrounds
05-favicons     SVG + PNG 16/32/48/180/192/512
06-watermarks   Video watermarks: full + symbol-only (white, soft shadow)
07-patterns     Tea-terrace pattern tiles, one per brand
08-badges       Category pills, creator verification badges, status tags
09-splash       Phone splash screens 1284x2778
10-social       Avatars, YouTube banners, 4:5 post templates, breaking-news card, music cover, poster, reel cover
tokens.css      Colours, type, spacing, motion, pills, badges, loaders (set data-brand on <html>)
motion.html     Open in a browser to see every logo intro and loader
brand-book/     The full written spec (Markdown)

Fonts (free, OFL, Google Fonts): Anek Latin, Anek Devanagari, Mukta.
All logos are outlined vector art: never retype them in a font.
