## Video watermarks

- **Full watermark:** symbol plus wordmark in **white at 85% opacity** with a soft shadow (0 2px 6px, black 45%), so it holds on bright and dark footage.
- **Symbol-only watermark:** white at 75%, for reels and anything under 720px wide.
- **Placement:** bottom-right, with a 3% inset (4% on vertical video, placed above the platform UI). Size it at 6–8% of the frame height.
- **Brand match:** a video shows the watermark of the product that publishes it. Never stack two watermarks. The master G appears only on company and cross-app content.
- **Premium content (CHIMAL films):** show the watermark for the first 10 seconds and the last 10 seconds only, not over the whole film.
