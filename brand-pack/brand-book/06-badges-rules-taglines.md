# Badges, do's and don'ts, and taglines

## Creator and content badges

| Badge | Colour | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified check | Gorkha Red disc, white tick | Verified identity. Valid in every app. |
| Verified Creator | Red pill with the G | Creators verified by the company (cross-app) |
| Verified Artist | Swara pink | Musicians with a claimed SWARA profile |
| Rising Creator | Ukaali mint | UKAALI creators on a growth streak (algorithmic, time-limited) |
| CHIMAL Original | Cinema amber | Films and series commissioned or premiered on CHIMAL |
| Verified Reporter | Press blue, white text | Accredited KHABAR contributors |
| LIVE | Red with a blinking dot | Live streams and premieres in progress |
| BREAKING | Press blue | KHABAR only |
| PREMIERE · NEW RELEASE · TRENDING | Product accent, Night text | Content states in their own apps |

**Category pills** (as on gorkhatv.site) use the tint fill, an accent border and accent text, and fill solid when selected. Sub-categories (News, Vlogs, Travel, Food…) use neutral Ridge chips, so the four product colours stay special.

## Do

- Let one product own each screen: one accent, one symbol.
- Use the Devanagari wordmark alongside the Latin one for Nepali-first audiences.
- Put the culture in the details: the peak inside the G, the terraces, the hill road.
- Keep Night as the stage and let photography and video bring the colour.
- Use the SVG files. Never retype the wordmarks.

## Don't

- Don't prefix products with "Gorkha TV" (no "Gorkha TV News").
- Don't use the flag, the khukuri, prayer flags, soldiers or big mountain illustrations.
- Don't make things gold to look premium. Cinema Amber belongs to CHIMAL alone.
- Don't recolour a symbol in another product's colour, or combine two symbols.
- Don't outline, bevel, add 3D effects or put gradients on the symbols, or stretch or rotate them.
- Don't put the symbols on busy photos without the watermark shadow or a Night scrim.
- Don't use gradients between brand colours, and don't use a neon glow.
- Don't use purple. It is not in the system.
- Don't use political colours or party symbols in any content graphics.
