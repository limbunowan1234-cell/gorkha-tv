Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GorkhaTV ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. GorkhaTV is operated by Adhar Limbu.

1. Information we collect

Account information

If you sign in, we use Google Sign-In. We receive and store your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique Google account identifier. If you set a custom display name or bio in your profile, we store that too.

Content you provide

Comments you post, videos you save to your list, channels you follow, and any information you submit through the "Submit Your Channel" or "Claim this Channel" forms (channel details, your name, your contact email, and any message you include).

Usage data

To power features like "Continue Watching" and personalized recommendations, and for our own internal analytics (page views, active sessions, watch time), we track activity tied to either your signed-in account or, if you're not signed in, an anonymous cookie that doesn't identify you personally.

Technical data

Like most websites, our servers process your IP address and browser information as part of normal operation — mainly to prevent abuse (e.g. rate-limiting excessive requests).

2. Cookies

GorkhaTV sets a session cookie when you sign in, and an anonymous cookie to power activity-based personalization if you're not signed in. In addition, our advertising partners — Google AdSense and Infolinks — and YouTube's embedded video player may set their own cookies to serve and measure ads or manage video playback. We don't control these third-party cookies; see the links in section 4 for their own policies.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the Service — sign-in, saved lists, follows, comments
  • To personalize what you see — e.g. "Continue Watching" and recommendation rows based on your activity
  • To moderate content and prevent abuse or spam
  • To understand overall site usage through internal, aggregated analytics

4. Third-party services

GorkhaTV uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google — for Sign-In and embedded YouTube video playback
  • Google AdSense — for advertising, which may use cookies to personalize ads based on your visits to this and other sites
  • Infolinks — for advertising, similarly
  • Cloudflare — our hosting and database provider, which processes data on our behalf to run the Service

5. Data sharing

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers listed above (to the extent needed to run GorkhaTV) or when required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and content data for as long as your account exists. If you request account deletion, we'll remove your personal data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain it for legal reasons.

7. Your rights

You can delete your own comments at any time. To request deletion of your account and associated data, or to ask what data we hold about you, contact us at support@gorkhatv.site.

8. Children's privacy

GorkhaTV is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the Contact Us page or at support@gorkhatv.site.
