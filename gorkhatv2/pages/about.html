About GorkhaTV

Darjeeling in Every Frame

GorkhaTV is a discovery platform for YouTube content from the hills of Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Kurseong, Mirik and Siliguri. We built it because the region's creators — vloggers, journalists, musicians, filmmakers, comedians — were putting out real, worthwhile content that was scattered across hundreds of individual YouTube channels with no single place to find it. GorkhaTV brings it together in one place, organized the way a viewer actually browses.

We don't host, upload, or own any video. Every video on GorkhaTV plays directly on YouTube — GorkhaTV only indexes public metadata (titles, thumbnails, descriptions) to help you discover it. All rights to the videos themselves remain with their original creators and YouTube.

For creators

If you make content from or about the Darjeeling region, you can submit your channel to be considered for GorkhaTV. Every submission is reviewed before it's added — we're not an automated aggregator that lists anything and everything, we're trying to keep this a genuinely useful, regionally-relevant collection.

Who runs GorkhaTV

GorkhaTV is operated by Adhar Limbu. If you have questions, feedback, or a channel you think belongs here, get in touch.
